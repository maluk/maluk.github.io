How New Hampshire payroll deductions work

New Hampshire has no state wage income tax withholding. Federal income tax withholding follows IRS Publication 15-T. Social Security and Medicare follow federal payroll rules.

Methodology and sources

We calculate a pay-period estimate using the selected pay frequency and withholding inputs. Missing YTD wages are projected from the pay date and current pay pattern.
